01 / The premise

A shortlist with receipts.

Kerala is not one place. It is a coast, a canal network, a tea-covered ridge, and a monsoon that rewrites the road every June. A good stay is usually the one that belongs to its setting.

So we started with the whole field: 7,813 listings across all 14 districts. We scored the evidence, corrected the geography, and kept the hundred places with both a strong record and a clear sense of place.

01

Evidence before enthusiasm

A 4.9 from 7,000 reviews outranks a 5.0 from eight. The ranking uses a Bayesian rating with a 50-review prior, then requires a minimum review count.

02

Terrain is part of the stay

Beach, backwater, hill, forest, lake, town. Every shortlisted place is plotted by longitude and elevation, so the shape of the list is visible.

03

Unknown is a value

Pool, food inclusion, and bonfire fields stay not-sure unless the source copy says so. We do not turn silence into a yes.

04 / The method

How a place earns its place.

No mystery score. The ranking is a weighted average with a prior, a review threshold, and a geography correction. The full method also lists what the data cannot tell you.

Read the full method ↗
  1. 01

    Read the field

    Start with every rated listing in the enriched dataset: 6,418 of 7,813 rows.

  2. 02

    Weight the evidence

    Bayesian rating with a 50-review prior. Minimum 30 reviews for the shortlist.

  3. 03

    Correct the map

    Reverse-geocode each shortlisted coordinate. The source district is query-based and wrong at some borders.

  4. 04

    Keep the shape

    Select the top 100, then plot them by longitude and elevation. The list should look like Kerala.

05 / Open data

The list is the dataset.

Everything behind the shortlist is public: the clean CSV, the enriched 48-field CSV, the enrichment report, and the field dictionary. Download it, check it, build on it.

Budget bands are estimates, not prices. Pool, food, and bonfire fields preserve uncertainty. Location images are cached from Wikimedia Commons with attribution.
